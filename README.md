## Hi there 👋
I am Dr. Deepak Verma, a Clinical Molecular Pathology Bioinformatics Analyst at Moffitt Cancer Center's Advanced Diagnostics Laboratory (ADL) in Tampa, Florida. My work focuses on clinical genomics, molecular diagnostics, bioinformatics pipeline development, somatic variant interpretation, and genomic reporting for oncology and precision medicine applications. I support the development and implementation of advanced molecular assays, including TSO500, MAP98, HAL Panel, and EPIC v2 Methylation Array, while contributing to clinically actionable genomic analyses and diagnostic workflows. Previously, I completed my postdoctoral training at Johns Hopkins Medicine, where my research centered on bioinformatics, cancer genomics, and translational microbiome science. With more than eight years of experience in next-generation sequencing (NGS), multi-omics data integration, and high-throughput data analysis, I specialize in integrating genomic, transcriptomic, epigenomic, and microbiome datasets to uncover biologically and clinically meaningful insights. My expertise includes cancer genomics, RNA-Seq, single-cell RNA-Seq, methylation analysis, metagenomics, clinical bioinformatics, and computational pipeline development using R, Python, Perl, Linux, QIIME2, Kraken2, MetaPhlAn, and HUMAnN. I hold a PhD in Cancer Informatics and Bioinformatics and have worked closely with clinicians, pathologists, and multidisciplinary research teams to advance precision medicine, translational bioinformatics, and genomics-driven healthcare. I am passionate about developing innovative, scalable, and clinically relevant solutions that bridge research discoveries and patient care through data-driven precision oncology and open science initiatives.
